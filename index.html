Open source

Vapor is free, open source, and runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no backend, and no tracking. Every calculation stays on your device.

  • Private by design Calculations never leave your browser.

  • Zero dependencies Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

  • MIT licensed Free to use, inspect, and contribute.
