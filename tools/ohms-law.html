Formulas used

    These calculations assume an ideal resistive circuit and do not replace battery safety checks. See the disclaimer.

    How it works

    For an ideal resistive DC circuit, voltage, current, resistance and power are related by Ohm's law and the power equations below.

    Voltage

    V = I × R

    Current

    I = V ÷ R

    Resistance

    R = V ÷ I

    Power

    P = V × I · P = V² ÷ R · P = I² × R
