How it works

Ingredient volumes are derived from your target nicotine strength, flavor percentage, and PG/VG goals. Nicotine base and flavor already contribute PG and VG, so additional PG/VG fill the remainder.

Nicotine base

Nicotine Base = (Final Volume × Target Nicotine) ÷ Base Strength

Flavor

Flavor = Final Volume × Flavor % ÷ 100

Additional PG

Additional PG = Target PG − PG from base − PG from flavor

Additional VG

Additional VG = Target VG − VG from base − VG from flavor
